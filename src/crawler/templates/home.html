URL Crawler

__AUTHOR__

Give it any URL. It returns the page's metadata, what kind of page it is, and what the page is about.

__EXAMPLES__
First request after a quiet period wakes the service; that takes about ten seconds.
raw JSON

  





How to read a result


    
  
  • status: success, blocked (the site refused) or error (something failed, or no content); reason says which, and is explained under the badge.
    • 
  
  • page_type comes from what the page declares (JSON-LD, Open Graph, price markup), then URL shape; other means nothing declared.
    • 
  
  • topics are keyphrases from the page text, ranked; score is that rank, not a probability.
